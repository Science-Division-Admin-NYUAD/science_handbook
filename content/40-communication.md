## Marketing


### Newsletters

#### Institutional Newsletters
NYUAD distributes newsletters for staff (Al Sho'la) and faculty (Faculty Gazette) that offer updates on upcoming events, important dates and deadlines, announcements, tailored professional development and funding opportunities, NYU global news, and other relevant information. New employees are automatically subscribed to receive these newsletters.

#### Science Division Newsletter
The monthly Science Division Newsletter delivers updates on the latest news, publications, and upcoming events relevant to the division. Content is primarily sourced from the Tamkeen Monthly Report (see Research Reporting below). New employees are automatically subscribed to this newsletter.

If you have feedback or contributions, contact Katie Stanworth at kls10@nyu.edu.

### Calendars and Email Groups

#### Events Calendar
The Science Events Calendar is kept up to date with all program seminars and events related to the division, including the annual Science Open House and Capstone Festival. Your program may also use additional calendars to schedule faculty meetings and log program teaching schedules; your program administrative coordinator will share these with you.

#### Division of Science General
During onboarding, your program administrative coordinator will add you to the CRB and ERB distribution groups. These lists receive program- and building-wide announcements relevant to the CRB and ERB communities on a weekly and daily basis. To share an announcement, please send the details to your program administrative coordinator or directly to erb.crb.announcements@nyu.edu.

Faculty members are also automatically added to the Science Faculty group, science-faculty-group@nyu.edu, which is used for updates from the Dean's Office. ERB researchers are added to the ERB Researchers group (erb-researchers-group@nyu.edu), where researchers and staff can share lab-based research questions and updates. Depending on your program and affiliation, your program administrative coordinator will add you to additional program-specific distribution groups.

## Research Reporting


#### Faculty 180
Each year, all NYU faculty from all of the NYU campuses report on their activities: teaching, research, and service. NYU uses this centralized system to generate reports, celebrate faculty successes and awards, and to minimize the information requests we send to faculty. You will submit your report using [Interfolio Faculty 180 (F180)](https://www.nyu.edu/faculty/teaching-and-learning-resources/faculty-advancement/interfolio-faculty-180.html). Entries are used to create research reports for the Division, and for use in the Annual Planning Report (APR) and accreditation documents. Faculty will receive an email copy of their submissions, which they can file for future reference.

#### Tamkeen Monthly Highlights
Each month, faculty will receive an email asking them to report on any highlights or activities from that month. This process is very important: it is a central request. The Office of Public Affairs and External Engagement generates a consolidated report for our investors, Tamkeen, featuring highlights, research activity, and accomplishments from the whole University. Content from the report is extracted for marketing and communication purposes, and featured across our communication channels, including the website, the monthly newsletter, social media, and — in applicable cases — the local/international media.

#### Press Releases
If you have a particular highlight or achievement that you think may be newsworthy (for example, something of regional or societal relevance; a discovery; an award; an impressive publication; a new grant), please inform Katie Stanworth with as much notice as possible. In the case of an upcoming paper, you must get in touch in advance of publication so the material can be reviewed confidentially, under embargo. A press release will then be drafted to coincide with the paper's release. Once published, you should also report it in the Tamkeen Monthly Highlights and upload it to Faculty 180.

If you want to share news or just need more information, contact Katie Stanworth at kls10@nyu.edu.
